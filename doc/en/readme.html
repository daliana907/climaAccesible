Accessible Weather (ClimaAccesible)

Version: 1.4  |  Author: Daliana  |  Compatibility: NVDA 2019.3 or later

Accessible Weather is a screen-reader friendly weather and forecasting add-on for NVDA. It allows users to check current weather conditions and detailed multi-day forecasts using simple shortcuts, without requiring accounts, API keys, or private tokens.

Keyboard Shortcuts

ShortcutFunction
NVDA + WAnnounces current weather conditions for the configured city
NVDA + Shift + WAnnounces multi-day extended weather forecast (1 to 14 days)
NVDA + Control + WOpens the accessible configuration dialog
NVDA + N > Tools > ClimaAccesibleAdd-on menu (Settings, Check conflicts, Documentation)

Getting Started

  1. Press NVDA + Control + W to open settings.
  2. In the Country combo box, select your country.
  3. Tab to Region / Department and select yours.
  4. Tab to City and pick your city.
  5. Check or uncheck which metrics you wish to hear.
  6. In the Extended Forecast section, pick how many days you want (1 to 14).
  7. Click Save.
Note: Over 50,000 cities are stored locally in the add-on. No Internet connection is needed to browse or configure your location, only when requesting real-time weather updates.
